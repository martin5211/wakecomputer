# Privacy Policy

This Alexa Smart Home skill is for personal use only and is not published to the Alexa Skills Store.

**Data collection:** This skill does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It only processes Alexa Smart Home directives (turn on/off) to control devices on your local network.

**Third-party services:** Requests are routed through AWS Lambda and ngrok to reach your local server. No data is stored by these services beyond standard operational logs.

**Contact:** This is a personal project with no commercial purpose.
